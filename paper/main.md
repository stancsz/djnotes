# A Structured DDJ-FLX4 Curriculum for Chinese-Speaking Beginner DJs

Curriculum architecture, practice progression, and evidence boundaries

## Abstract

This design report describes a Chinese-language beginner curriculum organized around the Pioneer DJ DDJ-FLX4 and rekordbox. The published sequence contains fourteen technical lessons and five career-oriented lessons, progressing from setup and cue monitoring through beatmatching, transitions, set preparation, performance contexts, and early professional practice. The curriculum connects procedural equipment knowledge with listening and practice. This is an instructional design description, not an educational efficacy study: no controlled comparison, learner outcome dataset, retention assessment, or representative accessibility evaluation is reported. We define a learner-centered evaluation plan and distinguish transferable DJ skills from controller-specific instruction.

## 1. Motivation and audience

The curriculum offers a connected learning path for Chinese-speaking beginners who own or are considering a DDJ-FLX4 [R1]. It uses rekordbox [R2] for concrete examples while treating listening, phrasing, selection, preparation, and performance judgment as skills that can transfer to other setups.

A complete sequence of lessons is an instructional artifact, not evidence that all learners progress at the same rate or achieve the intended skills.

## 2. Curriculum structure

The course contains nineteen lessons [R3]: fourteen technical lessons and five career-oriented lessons. The technical sequence covers setup, audio routing, gain, EQ, headphone monitoring, manual beatmatching, phrase structure, transitions, loops, hot cues, effects, harmonic mixing, livestream setup, practice, and set construction. The final lessons address early performance contexts and preparation.

The progression moves from a controllable practice environment toward contextual decisions. Learners establish the signal chain, practice timing and transitions, and then assemble and perform a set.

## 产品与研究原则

这套课程以 DDJ-FLX4 和 rekordbox 为具体练习环境，提供从设备设置、听觉训练、混音过渡到 set 准备和初次演出的中文学习路径。

- **学习者先行：** 每课说明练什么、听什么，以及如何判断练习是否完成。
- **迁移能力：** 设备操作是例子；对拍、乐句、选曲、set 准备与读场更具迁移性。
- **来源透明：** 设备专属行为以官方资料核对；课程建议不保证职业结果。
- **研究诚实：** 目前这是公开课程设计，没有学习者对照研究或长期技能数据。

## 3. Limitations and future work

There is no learner cohort, controlled teaching comparison, pre/post assessment, or verified long-term retention study in this draft. Career advice is contextual and is not a guarantee of paid work.

A first evaluation could assess a recorded transition before and after the course with blinded rubric ratings for beat alignment, phrase timing, level management, and continuity. Report participant experience, practice exposure, attrition, and retention.

## 4. Conclusion

The curriculum provides a structured starting point for DDJ-FLX4 beginners. Its educational value remains an empirical question for learner-centered evaluation.
